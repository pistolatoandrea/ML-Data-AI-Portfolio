# Food Influencer Market Trends in 2026

## Executive Summary

The food influencer market is poised for significant transformation by 2026, driven by evolving consumer preferences, technological advancements, and the increasing power of social media. Key trends include the rise of plant-based and green gastronomy, the integration of THC and CBD culinary products, and the influence of younger generations on food trends. Additionally, the role of influencers, both macro and micro, is becoming increasingly pivotal in shaping consumer behavior and brand strategies. This report delves into these trends, providing insights into the future landscape of the food influencer market.

## Key Findings

### 1. Plant-Based & Green Gastronomy

- **Continued Growth**: Plant-based alternatives are expected to maintain their momentum, with consumers increasingly interested in trying plant-based versions of traditional cuisines. This trend is supported by the growing popularity of hashtags like #plantbased and #vegan on social media platforms.
- **Product Expansion**: Brands are expanding their offerings to include high-quality plant-based products beyond meat substitutes, such as chicken, seafood, and dairy alternatives. Ready-to-eat meals and meal kits are also gaining traction.
- **Sustainability and Health**: The rise of plant-based diets is closely linked to a growing awareness of environmental sustainability and health-conscious lifestyles.

### 2. THC and CBD Culinary Products

- **Legalization Impact**: The increased legalization of THC in North America is driving the popularity of CBD and THC-infused products. Major food industry players are incorporating these components into widely consumed products.
- **Market Growth**: The cannabis food and beverage market is projected to grow at a compound annual growth rate of 15% over the next five years, with a surge in cannabis-related cookbooks and culinary content.
- **Consumer Benefits**: CBD is noted for its potential to enhance energy, focus, and alertness, appealing to consumers seeking functional wellness.

### 3. Influence of Younger Generations

- **Gen Z and Millennials**: These demographics are at the forefront of driving food trends, favoring novel flavors and functional wellness over traditional diet culture.
- **Cultural Exploration**: Younger consumers are using social media as a gateway to explore global cuisines, with a significant percentage expressing interest in trying foods from different cultures.
- **Participatory Global Eats**: Instagram predicts that Gen Z will continue to drive the trend of exploring and sharing culinary cultures online.

### 4. Social Media and Influencer Impact

- **Influencer Power**: Influencers, both macro and micro, are crucial in shaping consumer preferences and brand strategies. They offer authentic recommendations that resonate with their audiences.
- **Content Creation**: The rise of user-generated content (UGC) and the creator economy is transforming food and beverage marketing, with influencers creating their own brands and driving consumer engagement.
- **Visual Appeal**: Infographics and visually engaging content highlighting nutritional information are gaining popularity, helping consumers make informed choices.

### 5. Emerging Food Trends

- **DIY Culinary Culture**: Social media platforms are empowering individuals to become amateur chefs, with DIY food content becoming ubiquitous.
- **Global Flavor Fusion**: Bold flavor combinations and global fruits are trending, with social media driving the popularity of unique and shareable food items.
- **Seasonality and Cultural Moments**: Recipes and food trends that align with seasonal and cultural moments are expected to gain traction, offering fresher and more nutritious options.

## Conclusions

The food influencer market in 2026 will be characterized by a dynamic interplay of consumer preferences, technological advancements, and the growing influence of social media. Plant-based and green gastronomy will continue to thrive, driven by sustainability and health-consciousness. The integration of THC and CBD into culinary products will expand, offering new opportunities for innovation. Younger generations will play a pivotal role in shaping food trends, with a focus on cultural exploration and functional wellness. Influencers will remain central to marketing strategies, leveraging their authenticity and reach to drive consumer engagement. As the market evolves, brands must adapt to these trends to remain competitive and relevant in the ever-changing food landscape.
